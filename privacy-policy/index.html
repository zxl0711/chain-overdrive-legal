Privacy Policy — Digit Burst

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Digit Burst (数字风暴 / 數字風暴; the “Game”) and its independent developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handle information. It covers the Game on the Apple App Store (iOS) and, when released, Google Play (Android). The available features and service providers may differ by platform and version. Features described as conditional do not imply that they are active in every release. The Game is designed to be playable without a developer-operated account.

1. Local game data and information you provide

The Game stores settings, language preferences, game progress, local scores, gameplay records and related verification or pending leaderboard-submission records on your device. This information is used to run the Game, remember your preferences and maintain your records. Local data is not automatically a developer-operated online account. You can generally remove it by deleting the Game or clearing app data where supported; device or cloud backups may retain copies.

We do not require your name, home address, phone number, contacts or precise device location to play. If you email us for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send so that we can respond.

The Game offers an optional feedback link to forms hosted by Tally (tally.so). Opening a form leaves the native gameplay interface and loads a third-party webpage. The Game does not attach a game account ID, device identifier, game seed or gameplay record to that link, and does not send the feedback text to Firebase. If you submit feedback, we can view the text and any other information you voluntarily include. Tally may also process technical information related to visiting and submitting the form, such as browser and usage information, in accordance with its Privacy Notice. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Contact us if you would like us to locate or delete a submission where possible.

2. Advertising and advertising privacy choices

When ads are enabled, the Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's advertising partners. The planned initial iOS release uses occasional interstitial ads at a break between completed runs, when a player chooses to play again; it does not offer rewarded-revival ads. Other platform versions or future releases may have different ad formats, and this policy will be updated when necessary. A verified ad-removal purchase suppresses advertising requests made by the Game.

Google and its partners may process IP addresses and IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), app and ad interactions, impressions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals to serve, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Advertising data may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners. We do not operate an independent database of players' advertising identifiers. Google and its partners handle data under their applicable policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Where applicable, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present European consent notices and US state privacy choices. You may accept, refuse or manage available advertising choices in those notices as required by applicable law. Where UMP requires an in-app privacy-options entry point, it appears in the Game's Settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; contextual or other permitted ads may still appear. Advertising consent is separate from the optional gameplay-analytics and crash-report settings described below.

On iOS, if advertising involves tracking covered by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, the Game must obtain the required system permission before that tracking. You can change ATT permissions in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. ATT permission and UMP advertising choices are separate controls.

3. In-app purchases

The Game may offer a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes ads, including ads added in future versions. Apple processes iOS payments through StoreKit / Apple In-App Purchase; if offered on Android, Google processes payments through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment-card number. The Game checks verified transaction or entitlement status to grant and restore ad removal. Apple or Google may process purchases, transactions and fraud-prevention information under their respective policies.

4. Leaderboards and platform services

The iOS Game uses Apple Game Center for optional live leaderboards. When you are signed in to Game Center and a qualifying result is submitted, Apple may process your platform player identifier, display name, leaderboard identifier, result and related game context. Current leaderboard results include total damage for Score Attack or Weekly Challenge, or a Boss Challenge completion time; leaderboard context identifies the Boss. Public leaderboard entries, rankings and Game Center display names may be visible to other Game Center users under Apple's settings and visibility rules. The Game can also display fictional demonstration rankings; those are not real players.

If Google Play Games Services leaderboards are enabled in a future Android release, Google may similarly process your Play Games identifier, display name, results and game context under its policies. The Game does not currently operate its own player-account service, chat service or leaderboard backend. Signing out of a platform account or uninstalling the Game does not by itself delete a result already held by that platform; manage platform data and deletion requests through Apple or Google as applicable.

5. Optional gameplay analytics — Firebase Analytics

Supported iOS distribution builds include Google Firebase Analytics for product improvement. Gameplay analytics is off by default and is controlled separately by the optional “Share gameplay analytics” switch in the Game's Settings. The Game's custom gameplay events are sent only while this option is enabled and the service is available. Development, simulator and unsupported distribution builds do not use this production event pipeline.

Events can describe tutorial progress, starts and ends of runs, chosen game mode and difficulty, rules version, session duration, total damage, longest chain, successful counters, Boss outcomes and completion time, Weekly Challenge activity, the play-again action, ad requests and impressions, purchase or restore outcomes, and tapping the share action. These events are for understanding engagement, difficulty balance, reliability and monetization performance. A share tap is not proof that sharing was completed. We do not intentionally attach your name, email, feedback text or a developer account ID to these events.

When enabled, Firebase may also process SDK-generated information such as an app-instance or installation identifier, device and operating-system details, app version, sessions, approximate geographic information inferred from network data and event timestamps. This information may be pseudonymous rather than completely anonymous. The iOS Firebase configuration disables IDFV collection, analytics-based ad-personalization signals and automatic screen reporting; we do not use Firebase Analytics for cross-app advertising tracking. You can turn off gameplay analytics in Settings. The Game disables further analytics collection and requests a reset of its analytics data on withdrawal; this cannot recall data already sent to Google.

6. Optional crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

Supported iOS distribution builds also include Google Firebase Crashlytics to help diagnose crashes. Its “Share crash reports” choice is off by default and independent of gameplay analytics and advertising consent. Automatic crash-report uploading is disabled in the Game. With your opt-in, previously unsent reports stored on the device may be sent on a subsequent launch; without opt-in, the Game requests deletion of unsent reports when it makes the upload decision. Switching the option off stops further authorized sending and requests deletion of unsent reports, but cannot retract reports already transmitted or queued for transmission.

Crash reports may contain stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device model and technical characteristics, timestamps, session information and Crashlytics/Firebase installation identifiers. Such data helps diagnose and prioritize faults. We do not intentionally add your name or the contents of optional Tally feedback to crash reports. For Firebase service practices, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. Purposes, service providers and disclosures

We use information to operate gameplay and local records, provide platform leaderboards and purchase entitlements, display and measure ads when enabled, diagnose problems and prevent abuse, analyze optional gameplay metrics, and respond to feedback or support requests. Depending on the features you use, relevant recipients include Apple (Game Center and StoreKit), Google (AdMob, UMP, Firebase and potentially Google Play services) and Tally (feedback forms). These providers may act as service providers or handle certain information under their own privacy policies.

Where required by applicable law, processing is based on performance of the service you request, your consent for optional analytics or crash-report sharing and for advertising purposes that require consent, legitimate interests such as security and responding to requests where legally permitted, or legal obligations. You may withdraw consent for consent-based processing without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers. Advertising-related disclosures to Google and its partners may nevertheless qualify as a “sale,” “sharing” or use for targeted advertising under some US state privacy laws. Where those laws apply, use the relevant UMP privacy notice or in-app Privacy options to exercise available opt-out rights, or contact us. We may disclose information if lawfully required or necessary to protect the security of the Game and its users.

8. Retention and security

Local records generally remain until you remove app data or uninstall the Game, subject to device backups. Support emails and feedback are kept as long as reasonably necessary to respond, improve the Game or satisfy legal obligations; you may ask us to review or delete information we directly control. Data already provided to Apple or Google for purchases, rankings or advertising is subject to those providers' retention rules and your platform settings.

Firebase Analytics data is retained according to the configured Firebase/Google Analytics retention controls; Crashlytics reports and identifiers are subject to Firebase's published retention and deletion practices. Turning off either optional setting does not automatically erase all information previously received by a provider. Tally maintains its own technical records under its notice. We use standard security measures and encrypted connections where supported, but no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

You can change the optional gameplay-analytics and crash-report switches in the Game's Settings when those functions are available. Where required by UMP, Settings also provides “Privacy options” to review applicable advertising choices. iOS tracking permission is managed separately through the system Tracking settings. On Android, where supported, you can manage your advertising identifier and Google Play privacy preferences. You may also manage your Game Center or Google Play Games information and purchase history using the respective platform's tools.

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to processing of personal information, and to opt out of certain advertising uses. You may withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. For information controlled directly by the developer, email the contact below with enough details to locate the relevant record; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Some requests concerning platform accounts or advertising-network data may need to be made to Apple, Google or another responsible provider. We will respond to requests as applicable law requires.

10. Children

The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information directly to us. Additional protections or higher age thresholds may apply under local laws and the Game's store-audience settings. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us.

11. International processing

Depending on where you live and which features are available, Apple, Google, Tally or other relevant providers may process data in a different country from yours, subject to applicable transfer rules and the providers' safeguards. Tally's own notice describes its handling of information from form visitors.

12. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when Game features, third-party services, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will change when an updated policy is published. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent.
