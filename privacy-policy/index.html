Privacy Policy — Chain Overdrive

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chain Overdrive (the “Game”), published by the independent developer under the name Chain Overdrive (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles data. It applies to the Game distributed through Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). Advertising, gameplay analytics, in-app purchases and real platform leaderboards may be enabled in different releases. Not all features described below are available in every version. Where a feature is not enabled in your version, the data processing specific to that feature does not occur.

1. Information and game progress

The Game is designed to be playable without creating a developer-operated user account. Game settings, progress, scores and similar gameplay records may be stored locally on your device. You can generally remove locally stored Game data by clearing the app's storage (where supported) or uninstalling it, subject to your device's backup settings.

We do not ask you to provide your name, home address, phone number, contacts, or precise location to play the Game. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, and use it to respond to your request.

2. Advertising, if enabled

Some versions may show ads through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android or iOS, including interstitial ads between game sessions and optional rewarded ads. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may automatically process data such as device and advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information and ad performance data to deliver, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Ad-related data may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners under their policies. We do not receive your full advertising identifier as a user profile for direct identification.

Where required, we will request consent or offer applicable privacy choices before using data for personalized ads. On iOS, if the Game performs tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, we will request the required permission before tracking. Availability of personalized or non-personalized advertising depends on your location, settings and applicable law. See Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising data.

3. In-app purchases, if enabled

The Game may offer a one-time purchase to remove ads. If enabled, payments are processed by Google Play Billing on Android or Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) on iOS. We do not receive your full payment card number. The applicable platform provider may process payment, purchase and fraud-prevention information according to its policies; the Game may access purchase status or transaction tokens as needed to grant or restore an entitlement.

4. Leaderboards and platform services, if enabled

The Game may display demonstration rankings using fictional sample players and scores; demo data does not represent real players. If real leaderboards or achievements are enabled, platform services such as Google Play Games Services on Android or Apple Game Center on iOS may process your platform player identifier, display name, scores and related gameplay information. Scores and player display names submitted to a live leaderboard may be visible to other users according to the relevant service's settings and leaderboard visibility rules. We will update this policy if developer-operated leaderboards or additional ranking data processing are introduced. The Game does not currently provide a developer-operated chat or social network.

5. Gameplay analytics and event data (when enabled)

We may use gameplay analytics to understand how the Game performs and improve its balance, reliability and features. Events may include game starts and finishes, game mode and difficulty selected, scores and highest chains, boss encounters and defeats, daily challenge participation, ad impressions or rewarded-ad completion, and purchase or restoration outcomes. Analytics may also include app version, device and operating-system information, approximate region, timestamps, session identifiers and diagnostic events, depending on the analytics provider and configuration.

Where analytics is enabled, this information may be transmitted to our analytics service provider and associated with an app-instance or pseudonymous identifier. We do not require your real name or contact information to collect gameplay metrics. We will not use analytics identifiers for cross-app advertising tracking without meeting applicable consent and permission requirements. The precise analytics provider, identifiers and retention settings will be reviewed and reflected in our platform privacy disclosures before the relevant release.

12. Purpose, disclosure and sharing

We use data as needed to provide gameplay and save preferences, analyze gameplay and improve game balance where analytics are enabled, respond to support requests, support optional platform services, validate purchases, deliver and measure ads where enabled, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical problems. Relevant information may be processed by platform and service providers such as Google or Apple when their features are integrated. We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

6. Security and retention

We aim to minimize data use and rely on standard platform security measures, including encrypted network connections where supported. No system can guarantee absolute security. Local gameplay data remains on your device until removed, subject to operating-system backups. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to address your request or meet applicable legal obligations. If gameplay analytics is enabled, collected events will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for analytics and product improvement, according to the configured provider retention controls and applicable law. We will review the specific provider retention period before release. Third-party providers maintain their own retention and deletion practices under their policies.

7. Your choices and requests

You can remove local gameplay data by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling the Game. On Android, you can reset or limit use of the advertising ID where supported. On iOS, you can manage app tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can manage Google Play Games, Game Center and platform purchase information in the relevant Google or Apple services. Advertising consent and privacy controls may also be available within the Game where required. To ask us about information held directly by the developer, or request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email the address below. Include enough detail to locate your data, but do not send passwords or payment card information. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law, subject to any lawful retention requirements.

8. Children

The Game is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not intentionally request personal information directly from children. Age-based restrictions and additional protections may apply depending on the Game's published audience settings and applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us.

9. International processing

Depending on your location and the services enabled, providers such as Google or Apple may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable privacy laws and the providers' safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Game features, SDKs, or legal requirements. We will update the effective date on this page when changes are published. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels where required.

11. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact the developer of Chain Overdrive at chain.overdrive.2026@gmail.com.
